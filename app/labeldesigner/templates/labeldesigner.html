The PDF has pages. Each page you give, e.g. 1-3, 5 or all, is printed as a label of its own, of several PDFs the same pages of each. The preview shows the first.

Templates insert content when the label is printed:

  • {{'{{datetime:}}'}} - the current date and time. The format uses strftime codes, e.g. %H:%M:%S %d.%m.%Y.
  • {{'{{counter[:]}}'}} - a number that counts up, starting at <start> (default 1). When you print several labels at once, each gets the next number.
  • {{'{{uuid'}}}} - a random UUID.
  • {{'{{short-uuid'}}}} - a shorter random UUID.
  • {{'{{env:}}'}} - the environment variable <var>, if it is listed in LABEL_TEMPLATE_ENV_VARS.
  • {{'{{random[:][:shift]}}'}} - a random string of <len> characters (default 64, at most 10000). shift shifts it around to fill gaps.
